# About Salesforce Data 360

Ingest, harmonize, unify, and analyze streaming and batch data with Data 360. Then use that data to unlock meaningful and intelligent experiences across Customer 360 applications and beyond.

Note

As of October 14, 2025, Data Cloud has been rebranded to Data 360. During this transition, you may see references to Data Cloud in our application and documentation. While the name is new, the functionality and content remains unchanged.

## How Does It Work?

![Data 360 data flow chart](https://sf-zdocs-cdn-prod.zoominsoftware.com/tdta-data_cloud-260-0-0-production-enus/a5d0a6a2-ffc5-455f-8207-c14ddf614626/data_cloud/images/Data360_HowDoesItWork_022326_zh.png)

*   Connect all your data sources whether your data is unstructured or structured data.
*   Ingest batch or streaming data.
*   Connect data using zero copy data federation.
*   Prepare your data through transformation and data governance features.
*   Harmonize your data to a standard data model.
*   Unify data with identity resolution rulesets.
*   Query and analyze data using insights.
*   Use AI to predict behavior.
*   Segment your data and activate to use in various channels to create personalized experiences.
*   Analyze your data using supported analytic tools.
*   Output data to multiple sources to act on data based on your business needs.
*   Ground your data for use with Agentforce.
*   Continue to review, measure, and optimize data.

## Customize Your Data 360 Experience

Data 360 is built to scale, allowing you to bundle features as needed. Some Data 360 features are offered as add-on licenses, and others have usage limitations, though many limits can be adjusted to meet your business needs. Learn about current [Data Cloud Limits and Guidelines](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=data.c360_a_limits_and_guidelines.htm&language=en_US&type=5) and then work with your account executive to find a solution that meets your goals.
