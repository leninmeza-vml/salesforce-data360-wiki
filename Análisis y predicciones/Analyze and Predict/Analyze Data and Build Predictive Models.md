## About Tableau Semantics

Tableau Semantics is the powerful, AI-infused semantic layer integrated into Data 360 that translates your data into familiar business terms and logic. Use Tableau Semantics with any data in your organization that’s accessible through Data 360. When you create semantic models and semantic definitions on top of Data 360, you can get better data that’s associated with your business goals. Test your semantic models and copy generated SQL queries to access data in other analytics or business intelligence (BI) tools.

To sum up, with Tableau Semantics, you can centralize trusted data and expand how it’s used across Salesforce applications, analytics tools, and AI. Meanwhile, built-in AI assistance reduces IT overhead by eliminating extra tools and manual processes.

## Einstein Studio: Your AI Home Base

Your business has data that can help AI predict future outcomes and drive decision-making at your organization. But how do you enable AI to identify, surface, and visualize insights within your Data 360 data? Enter Einstein Studio, your AI home base within Data 360 for accessing all your data, regardless of data type or where it lives. Using clicks not code, Einstein Studio lets you easily augment your business intelligence with AI, helping you quickly create and use generative models, predictions, and improvements.

With Einstein Studio, you can create an AI model, for example, that analyzes customer behavior to predict which prospects are most likely to purchase (Propensity to Buy Scoring), so that sales teams can focus their efforts effectively. Einstein Studio makes these predictions easy, accessible, and trusted–Einstein Studio is integrated directly into your Data 360 environment and therefore sits under the Einstein Trust Layer.

Einstein Studio offers you three ways to use AI models.

*   **Create predictive AI models from scratch.** Business users without specialized technical expertise can build, deploy, and activate machine learning models directly on their existing Data 360 data.  
    
*   **Bring your own model (BYOM).** Connect predictive AI models built in external platforms (such as Amazon SageMaker, Google Cloud Vertex AI, or Databricks) directly with Data 360. Once connected, predictions from these external models can be seamlessly consumed across Salesforce, bringing customer insights into every touchpoint.  
    
*   **Bring your own large language model (BYOLLM).** This extends the BYOM concept to generative AI, allowing you to add a foundation model hosted on an external platform and connect it with Einstein Studio to power your generative AI solutions.  
    

Learn more about machine learning models in [Machine Learning Predictions: Quick Look](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/machine-learning-predictions-quick-look).
